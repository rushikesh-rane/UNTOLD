# Brag Plan: UNTOLD

## What is this app?
UNTOLD is an interactive storytelling platform where writers release stories in parts and readers form theories and predictions while waiting for the next reveal.

## The angle
Treat the missing piece like a cinematic mystery: let the real Discover feed draw us in, show that the next chapter is deliberately hidden until its scheduled release, then reveal the reader experience around that gap—make a prediction, compare theories, and wait for the truth. The video borrows the product’s own editorial type and story-card hierarchy in the requested charcoal palette, preserving the violet brand accent and adding sparse blue edge light, with dramatic pacing rather than generic tech spectacle.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
On a deep-charcoal stage with restrained violet and blue edge light, a thin rule draws across the screen; “Every story has a missing piece.” lands in the real Playfair-like serif, followed by “UNTOLD” as the story feed slides into view.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Actual Discover feed treatment: For you / Trending / New tabs, mystery story card, chapter preview and scheduled reveal indicator.
- Creator preview’s real locked chapter treatment: a soft obscured page beneath “This part is still hidden,” followed by the reader’s “THE NEXT PART” countdown. This represents controlled reveal, not a fabricated reader blur feature.
- Reader prediction UI: “Pick what happens next.” with a selected answer.
- Theory room UI: a reader theory with a reply, likes and discussion affordances.

## Outro / punchline
“Read a little. Guess a lot. Discover the truth.” resolves into the UNTOLD wordmark and “Every story has a missing piece.”

## User flow worth showing
Discover a story → read the available part and wait for its scheduled reveal → choose a prediction and share a theory with other readers.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: quiet, premium mystery trailer for an editorial storytelling product
- Interpretation: Dramatic reveals, steady camera movement, soft crossfades, restrained impact accents, and readable holds. Keep the product UI as the visual centerpiece.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0B0D12 with #12151C transitions
- Panels: #191D27; accent: restrained violet #A78BFA and electric blue highlights
- Text: #F5F5F7; secondary text #A1A7B5
- Display font: Playfair Display (project’s configured font), with Georgia fallback
- Body font: DM Sans in the project; video uses Hyperframes’ bundled Inter as a close rendering substitute
- Strongest visual element: dark editorial story cards and serif headlines, contrasted with violet lock/reveal markers and rare blue accents

## Share copy (draft)
UNTOLD. Read a little. Guess a lot. Discover the truth.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support
- Music: Happy Beats / Business Moves Vol. 12 by Sascha Ende (ende.app), locally bundled
- Music treatment: Start at 0, understated at 0.30 volume, build gently under the reveals, fade over the closing wordmark.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset, 109.96 BPM. Target 8.74s for the obscured chapter reveal and 17.47s for the closing brand resolve; keep natural copy holds.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; cue the violet/blue ambient edge glow to swell at the reveal and brand landing.
- SFX posture: sparse, cinematic, motion-matched; two soft reveals and one restrained final impact.
- Audio-coupled moments: feed card arrival; obscured chapter lift; prediction selection; closing wordmark.
- Restraint rule: no loud impacts over the reading UI; no voiceover.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The missing piece — 3s
Deep charcoal with restrained violet and blue edge light; the exact line “Every story has a missing piece.” The UNTOLD mark enters as the Discover surface comes into focus. Text holds long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: yes — rule, headline, then mark in sequence.
Audio intent: low cinematic bed begins, a quiet swell under the title.
Audio-coupled idea: gentle title arrival.
Transition mood: dramatic soft reveal → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Find the story — 4s
Recreate the actual feed card hierarchy and visible tabs (“For you / Trending / New”) with a fictional editorial mystery card. Show a chapter preview row and the scheduled release chip. Avoid implying actual story data or real people.
Sequential/interaction: yes — feed enters, then card and reveal chip settle.
Audio intent: bed gains a little pulse; a light card placement accent.
Audio-coupled idea: story card lands on a beat, detail follows.
Transition mood: soft crossfade with a restrained push → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The part stays hidden — 4s
Show the Creator Studio preview’s actual obscured chapter treatment and lock overlay, then cut to the reader’s real “THE NEXT PART” countdown and “REVEAL PENDING” status. On-screen phrase: “Until then, the words stay hidden.” (shortened from the reader copy.)
Sequential/interaction: yes — veil draws over chapter text, lock/reveal status appears.
Audio intent: restrained low swell, one soft reveal accent.
Audio-coupled idea: obscured layer eases away enough to reveal the locked state, not the chapter text.
Transition mood: dramatic wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Guess together — 5s
Recreate the actual prediction card with “Pick what happens next.” and two choices; animate one selected state. Slide into the real Theory Room heading and one fictional theory plus a visible reply affordance.
Sequential/interaction: yes — one prediction is selected, then a theory card and reply indicator arrive. Keep settled text on screen for at least 1s.
Audio intent: music opens warmly; subtle selection click, no chatter or voices.
Audio-coupled idea: click aligns to the selected prediction; discussion card follows on the next roomy pulse.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — UNTOLD — 4s
Pull back from the reader UI into the brand wordmark. Exact product lines: “Read a little. Guess a lot. Discover the truth.” and “Every story has a missing piece.” Final mark holds cleanly.
Sequential/interaction: yes — tagline then brand line.
Audio intent: one restrained closing impact, music fades out.
Audio-coupled idea: final wordmark lands near 17.47s, with a settled hold.
Transition mood: soft fade to near-black

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic, warm, restrained.
**Audio summary:** A quiet cinematic bed swells through the locked reveal and reader participation, then resolves with one soft brand hit and a clean fade.
